Today's New York Times profiles Nashaa, one of UCLA’s Bollywood-fusion dance teams and an inspiration for the new Netflix comedy “Best of the Best.” Through photos documenting auditions, late-night rehearsals and competitions, the piece explores how the team gives South Asian students a way to connect with their culture and build community. Read more about UCLA in today’s New York Times, Southern California News Group, and other outlets.
