NPR spoke with UCLA ecologist Morgan Tingley about how birds may provide valuable data on the toxic effects of wildfires on both animals and humans. Tingley, a leader in the burgeoning field of smoke ecology, joins other scientists in rethinking traditional approaches to studying smoke, especially as the world experiences more wildfires. Read more about UCLA in today’s Fortune, Los Angeles Times and other outlets.
