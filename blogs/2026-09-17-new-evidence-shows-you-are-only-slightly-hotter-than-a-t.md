The Los Angeles Times covered a UCLA study that measured Tyrannosaurus rex’s temperature for the first time and found it to be 97 degrees Fahrenheit, similar to humans. Scientists used a new method to measure body temperature using the remains of “Thomas,” the resident T. rex at the Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County.
