The New York Times spoke with Dr. Edward Garon, director of thoracic oncology at the UCLA Health Jonsson Comprehensive Cancer Center, about the prognosis for Pope Leo XIV, who was diagnosed with lung cancer on Tuesday. Garon joined other experts to explain the medical profile of lung adenocarcinoma and what next steps might be for the first American pope.
