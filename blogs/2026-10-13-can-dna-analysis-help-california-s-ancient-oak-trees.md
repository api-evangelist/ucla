KCRW reported that UCLA scientists and the Nature Conservancy are using genetic analysis to identify oak trees best equipped to survive California’s increasingly hot, dry climate. UCLA biologist Ryan Buck said researchers hope to use the findings to identify more resilient trees whose acorns and seedlings could help restore struggling oak populations across the state. Read more about UCLA in today’s Los Angeles Business Journal, New York Times and other outlets.
