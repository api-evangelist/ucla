Inc. magazine highlighted a UCLA-led study that found that signs of accelerated brain aging in younger and middle-aged adults were associated with poorer cognitive performance, greater depressive symptoms and differences in gut bacteria and metabolites. The study's lead author, Kanhao Zhao, now at Stanford University, conducted the research while at UCLA.
