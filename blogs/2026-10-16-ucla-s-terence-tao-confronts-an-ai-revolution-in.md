The New York Times profiled UCLA mathematician and Fields Medal recipient Terence Tao, who has emerged as a leading voice in the debate over AI’s rapidly growing role in mathematical research and its impact on younger scholars. The article also featured UCLA professors Andrea Bertozzi and Dimitri Shlyakhtenko, as well as Tao’s graduate students Ben Goldman, Siddharth Mulherkar and Arian Nadjimzadah. Read more about UCLA in today’s Washington Post, CNN and other outlets.
