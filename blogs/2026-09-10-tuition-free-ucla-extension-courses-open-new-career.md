The Los Angeles Sentinel reported on a new partnership between UCLA Extension and the SoLa Foundation offering tuition-free courses to South Los Angeles residents. Held at the foundation’s Crenshaw campus, courses in fields including AI, entrepreneurship and project management aim to provide residents with marketable skills and new pathways to career advancement. Read more about UCLA in today’s Bloomberg Law, USA Today and others.
