The Washington Post reported on new UCLA findings indicating there has not yet been an AI-powered spike in unemployment among recent college graduates, based on statistics from June to August. The paper is among the first attempts to examine the impact of AI on the graduating class of 2026, whose students gained access to ChatGPT by the end of their first college semester. Read more about UCLA in today’s San Francisco Chronicle, Associated Press and others.
